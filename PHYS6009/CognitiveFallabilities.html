First formulated by Peter Cathcart Wason in 1966, this puzzle is a foundational tool in cognitive psychology used to demonstrate our systematic blind spots in falsificationism.

Instructions:

Interactive calibration test

The Spiegelhalter Calibration Game

Adapted from David Spiegelhalter's paper (Nature, Dec 2024). This game tests your self-calibration. By punishing overconfidence and rewarding honest uncertainty assessment, your final Brier-derived score reveals if you suffer from the illusion of knowledge.

Instructions:

1. Pick the answer (A or B) you believe is most likely correct.

2. Rate your confidence on a scale of 5 to 10. (5 means complete guess/50%, 10 means absolutely certain/100%).
